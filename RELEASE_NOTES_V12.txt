UDC V12 UNIVERSAL — RELEASE NOTES

1. Original V11 interface is preserved; only version text/status messaging was updated.
2. The bundled B.S. 1000A:1961 Abridged English Edition remains the primary authority.
3. History/geography country mappings are handled by edition-specific semantic rules before generic matching.
4. The previous emergency fallback "0" was removed. V12 returns VERIFICATION REQUIRED when evidence is insufficient instead of asserting an unrelated class.
5. seed-udc.json was corrected to match the bundled 1961 authority.
6. The engine explicitly prevents modern UDC/DDC substitution.
7. AI providers remain optional; when configured, retrieved pages from the bundled source are supplied to the model.
8. For universal coverage beyond the bundled edition, import/use the user's legally obtained/licensed UDC reference and keep edition provenance explicit.

Important:
For this exact 1961 Abridged edition, "History of China" is 951. A modern UDC construction such as 94(510) must not be silently substituted into this project.
