V12 COMPLETE UNIVERSAL POLICY

1. Analyze complete title meaning first.
2. Identify the head subject/phenomenon.
3. Detect explicit facets: place, language, time, document form, process, material, persons.
4. Build UDC notation only from supported UDC syntax and reference evidence.
5. An alphabetical-index hit can never override the head concept merely because a single word matches.
6. Country names are place facets, not automatic subjects.
7. No DDC. No random nearest-number selection. No title-only patch list as the primary classifier.
8. Exact/source-backed > semantic construction > broad subject fallback.
9. Example: Religious unrest in India must be analysed as unrest + India, not as an unrelated entry containing the word unrest.
10. For current UDC syntax, 94(410) is the Consortium's documented pattern for History of United Kingdom; common place auxiliaries are synthetically appended to subject numbers.
