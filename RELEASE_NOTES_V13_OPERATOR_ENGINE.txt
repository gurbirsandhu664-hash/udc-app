V13 — UNIVERSAL UDC OPERATOR ENGINE

- Added semantic operator selection for compound titles.
- + is reserved for coordination/addition of independent UDC subjects.
- : is used for simple relation/application phrasing such as “X in Y” when both concepts are known UDC classes.
- Both component concepts must resolve to known UDC concepts before synthesis.
- No keyword-only compound numbers are generated.
- Existing exact/current authority rules run before this generic operator engine.
- Example: Higher Education and Computers -> 378+004 (coordination).
- Example: Computers in Higher Education -> 378:004 (semantic relation candidate).
- Example: Music and Entertainment -> 78+79.
- Example: Harvesting of wheat and maize remains governed by the existing verified construction 633.11+633.15:631.55.

UDC Consortium defines + as Coordination/Addtion and : as Simple relation; this engine follows that distinction.
