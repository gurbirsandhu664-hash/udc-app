V11 — semantic edition-first fix

The classifier now has an edition-specific semantic layer BEFORE generic keyword matching and BEFORE optional AI. This prevents a country/history title from selecting an unrelated number merely because a word such as “history” or “China” occurs elsewhere in the uploaded text.

Important authority rule:
The bundled B.S. 1000A:1961, 3rd Edition Revised 1961 is the authority. Therefore History of China and History of Korea are both 951 in this edition because the printed 1961 history schedule says “951 China and neighbours. Korea”. Modern UDC constructions such as 94(510) are not substituted.

Geography titles are similarly resolved against the printed 1961 geography schedule (e.g. 915.1 China and neighbours; 915.4 India; 914.4 France; 917.3 United States; 919.4 Australia).

For titles outside these deterministic schedule rules, the classifier continues to use uploaded-source evidence retrieval and optional Gemini/Groq semantic synthesis. No DDC is used.
