V45 ULTRA — STABLE RELEASE
==========================

Release: 45.0.0
Status: Stable deterministic release
Classification policy: UDC ONLY — Never DDC

FIXED
-----
1. Knowledge Metaphysics and Logic -> 001+11+16
   001 = Science and knowledge in general; 11 = Metaphysics; 16 = Logic,
   epistemology and theory of knowledge; + coordinates the subjects.

2. Handbook of Education, Science and Technology -> 37+5/6(035)
   37 = Education; + coordinates the education subject with 5/6; / is the
   consecutive extension; (035) is the form auxiliary for handbooks/manuals.

3. Handbook of Science and Technology -> 5/6(035)

4. Economy of India -> 330(540)

5. Dictionary of Language and Literature -> 80(038)

6. Systematic Zoology / Handbook of Systematic Zoology -> 592/599

7. History/Geography/Constitution/Indian Literature/Philosophy/Art entries
   retain their deterministic UDC answers and relevant auxiliaries.

8. Harvesting/crop titles retain deterministic relation notation instead of
   changing to a random broad class.

STABILITY
---------
- Exact title keys are checked before generic keyword rules.
- Punctuation/case variations are normalized.
- No random title-to-title answer selection is used in the client classifier.
- The old misleading 00/0 fallback is not emitted for unknown titles.
- Unknown titles show a verification state rather than inventing a UDC number.
- Existing UI/answer concept and V45 ULTRA naming are preserved.
- VERSION.txt is intentionally not included; this release uses RELEASE_NOTES.txt.

AUTHORITY
---------
The app uses public UDC Summary information as an abridged-style reference.
The UDC Consortium's complete UDC Online/Master Reference File is separate
licensed material. Exact MRF-level classification should be checked against
an authorized UDC source.
